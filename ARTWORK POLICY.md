# Artwork and audio preference

The user does not want AI-generated artwork or audio assets in this game.

Use the installed RPG Maker MV artwork, or human-created assets the user supplies or explicitly authorizes. Do not generate images for this project.

All 84 PNG files currently under the game's `img` folder are unmodified copies of the installed RPG Maker MV assets. The proposed AI-generated creature was removed from the project and is not loaded by the game.

The title layout, lighting masks, scanlines, captions, and other interface effects are drawn by game code. Characters, furniture, and environment tiles use MV assets.

Audio now comes from the installed RPG Maker MV library and the installed Horror Soundscapes pack by ayato sound create. The earlier synthesized prototype sounds have been removed from the playable project. No generated voices, music, or sound effects are used.

Imported Horror Soundscapes files are unchanged copies, with source paths and hashes in `licenses/audio-manifest.json`. Their required attribution appears in the title-screen Credits and in `CREDITS.md`. No new purchase was made.
