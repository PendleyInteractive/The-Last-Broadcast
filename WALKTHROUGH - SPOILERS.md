# Prototype walkthrough — spoilers

1. In the Vale house, investigate the paper on the central desk to obtain June's cassette. Read the birthday photograph note at the east side: **0412**.
2. Leave through the south door. On Ash Street, enter the east door to Mrs Bell's house.
3. Talk to Mrs Bell. Open the cabinet/desk at the east side with **0412**. Take the school key.
4. Return to Ash Street and enter the north gate. Read the instructions; enter the northwest classroom door.
5. Read the marked register. Its marked rows in numerical order are **137**. Enter that at the cabinet to unlock the basement.
6. Return to the school entrance and take the northeast door. Cross the basement to its northeast exit. Run or use a cabinet if needed.
7. In hospital reception, read the maintenance memo. Enter **241** at the breaker, then enter Ward Zero.
8. Read the casualty list and signed refusal. The refusal gives **23:10**, useful later. Leave through the northeast maintenance stairs.
9. At floodgate access, set the west valve CLOSED and the east valve OPEN. Activate the central control and enter the pump room.
10. Read the emergency master tape, then leave through the surface exit.
11. In the altered house, collect all three anchors: paper boat, height marks, and lullaby. Enter the refuge through the northeast door.
12. Talk to Daniel's mother, read the names, then enter the radio station.
13. Confront Thomas in the lobby. Cross the pursuit corridor to the archive.
14. Unlock the master archive with **2310**. A duplicate station log nearby provides the time if you missed the hospital clue.
15. Enter the transmitter. Save at the recorder on the west side. Interact with the broadcasting console to choose Remember, Forget, or Stay.

All clues remain available in Field notes after being collected. The prototype has no collectible requirement for the Remember ending.
